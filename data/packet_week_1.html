The draft produced two genuine cornerstones in Lamar Jackson and Justin Jefferson plus a strong WR corps (Brian Thomas, Jameson Williams, Luther Burden), but the team enters Week 1 with two effectively dead roster spots: Josh Jacobs is on the NFL's Commissioner's Exempt List with a Nov. 17 court date, and A.J. Brown suffered a right ankle injury in New England's already-completed Wednesday opener. The result is a title-caliber top end with a genuinely thin, committee-based backfield. Title path runs through fixing RB via trade, not via this week's weak waiver pool.
